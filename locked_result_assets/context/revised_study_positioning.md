# Revised Study Positioning

## Most appropriate study positioning

The study is best positioned as a nationwide longitudinal prognostic stratification study in middle-aged and older adults, focused on how current multidomain burden and pre-landmark multidomain change relate to subsequent ADL disability risk.

It should not be positioned as:

- a mechanism study
- a causal pathway study
- a definitive latent taxonomy study
- a pure machine-learning prediction paper

## What pre-disability state can and cannot mean here

Safest acceptable wording includes:

- a clinically interpretable pre-disability high-risk phenotype
- consistent with an early pre-disability state
- may help identify older adults already on a higher-risk disability pathway

Wording that should be avoided includes:

- validated autonomous pre-disability state
- distinct disease-like state
- burden-independent latent state
- clinically ready classification entity

The reason is straightforward: Stage 5 showed that Emerging retained information after adjustment for current burden, but its effect size clearly attenuated, and Stage 8 showed that the trajectory structure remained strongly anchored to physical limitation.

## How adversity should be positioned

Adversity should be downgraded to a secondary etiologic-context marker or upstream vulnerability marker. It should be framed around association with adverse phenotype membership and disability-pathway risk, not as a co-equal headline or near-term mortality story.

## Most important Discussion boundary

The key Discussion boundary is that trajectory-related information is valuable mainly as modest refinement and clinical compression of multidomain risk, not as a replacement for current multidomain burden assessment.

This boundary can be broken into four statements:

1. current burden dominates prediction
2. explicit change adds modest independent information
3. hard phenotype is clinically useful but information-losing
4. Emerging is meaningful because it is high-risk and interpretable, not because it is a fully independent latent state

## Recommended central message

Current multidomain burden at the landmark remained the dominant predictor of incident ADL disability, while pre-landmark multidomain change provided modest incremental refinement. The trajectory phenotype was most useful as a clinically interpretable subgroup representation, especially for identifying Emerging multidomain decline as a high-risk pre-disability phenotype, rather than as the strongest standalone predictive form.
